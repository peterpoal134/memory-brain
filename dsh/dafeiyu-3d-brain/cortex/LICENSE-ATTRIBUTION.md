# Human cortical surface and derived surface filaments

Original author: **Anderson Winkler**.

Original work: [Brain for Blender](https://brainder.org/research/brain-for-blender/).

Source archive: https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/brainder/software/brain4blender/smallfiles/pial_Full_obj.tar.bz2

Original and derived mesh assets are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Changes: converted Wavefront OBJ to indexed binary positions and faces, centered coordinates and uniformly scaled them to 3.65 units; generated decorative surface-following filaments using edges of the original mesh. The display scene applies an additional artistic transform of 1.15 on X and 0.94 on Y, shaders and colors. These visualizations do not indicate the anatomical locations of the user's memories.

Additional changes for the radiant hologram: generated 620 smoothed, surface-following decorative strands per hemisphere from the mesh adjacency; runtime decorative bundles sample the same surface around real data-node anchors. The cortical mesh serves as an invisible depth scaffold beneath particles and luminous strands. Memory placement and decorative strands are artistic, not anatomical or scientific connections.

This license notice applies to lh.bin, rh.bin, lh.fibers.bin, rh.fibers.bin, lh.hologram.bin, rh.hologram.bin and derived cortical geometry. No endorsement by the original author is implied.

Geometry can be regenerated from the source archive with scripts/prepare-cortex.py in the preview project. The current luminous strands are generated by scripts/prepare-holographic-fibers.py. Metadata is recorded in metadata.json and hologram-metadata.json.
